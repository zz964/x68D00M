PCM Driver Stop Mechanism Test Results
======================================
Date: 2026-03-28
Test program: pcm8a_test.c (pcm8test.x)
Tone: 3-second ADPCM square wave, stop attempted at 500ms

Legend:
  STOPPED = tone silenced at 500ms (desired behavior)
  FULL    = tone played all 3 seconds (stop had no effect)
  NO PLAY = sound did not start at all

======================================================================
PCM8A v1.02
======================================================================

PART A: Sound started via IOCS ADPCMOUT
  A1  Full play (baseline)          -- FULL (expected)
  A2  ADPCMMOD(0)                   -- FULL
  A3  Trap #2 $00B0 (stop ch 0)    -- FULL
  A4  Trap #2 $0100 (reset all)    -- FULL
  A5  Trap #2 $0103 (kill driver)  -- STOPPED

PART B: Sound started via trap #2 $0000 (direct channel 0)
  B1  Full play (baseline)          -- NO PLAY
  B2-B5                             -- NO PLAY (can't test, nothing playing)

PART C: Mixed
  C1  Trap $0000 + stop ch 0-7     -- NO PLAY
  C2  Two ADPCMOUT + $0103 kill    -- STOPPED

Conclusion: PCM8A can only stop ADPCMOUT sounds by killing the entire
driver ($0103). Per-channel stops and resets have no effect. Trap #2
direct channel start ($000x) does not work at all.

======================================================================
PCM8PP (Mercury Unit driver)
======================================================================

PART A: Sound started via IOCS ADPCMOUT
  A1  Full play (baseline)          -- FULL (expected)
  A2  ADPCMMOD(0)                   -- STOPPED
  A3  Trap #2 $00B0 (stop ch 0)    -- FULL
  A4  Trap #2 $0100 (reset all)    -- STOPPED
  A5  Trap #2 $0103 (kill driver)  -- FULL (opposite of PCM8A!)

PART B: Sound started via trap #2 $0000 (direct channel 0)
  B1  Full play (baseline)          -- FULL (plays, quiet volume)
  B2  ADPCMMOD(0)                   -- STOPPED (quiet)
  B3  Trap #2 $00B0 (stop ch 0)    -- FULL (quiet)
  B4  Trap #2 $0100 (reset all)    -- STOPPED (quiet)
  B5  Trap #2 $0103 (kill driver)  -- FULL (quiet)

PART C: Mixed
  C1  Trap $0000 + stop ch 0-7     -- FULL (quiet)
  C2  Two ADPCMOUT + $0103 kill    -- FULL

Conclusion: PCM8PP responds to ADPCMMOD(0) and $0100 (reset) for both
ADPCMOUT and trap #2-started sounds. Per-channel stop ($00Bx) does NOT
work. Kill ($0103) does NOT work (opposite of PCM8A). Direct channel
start ($000x) works but at reduced volume (different d1 parameter
format expected).

======================================================================
Summary: What actually stops sounds
======================================================================

                    | PCM8A  | PCM8PP
  ------------------+--------+--------
  ADPCMMOD(0)       |  no    |  YES
  $00Bx (per-ch)    |  no    |  no
  $0100 (reset)     |  no    |  YES
  $0103 (kill)      |  YES   |  no

The two drivers have OPPOSITE stop behavior. There is no single stop
method that works on both.

Recommended approach for Doom:
  - Detect which driver is loaded
  - PCM8A:  use $0103/$0104 (kill/reactivate) to stop sounds
  - PCM8PP: use ADPCMMOD(0) to stop sounds
  - Or: use each driver's native API (Mercury uses TRAP #2 with
    per-channel volume, PCM8A uses IOCS ADPCMOUT interception)
